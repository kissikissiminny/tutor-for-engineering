# Mechanics Reference Sheet (Ground Truth)

## 1. Physical Constants
- Standard Earth Gravity: $g = 9.81\ \text{m/s}^2$ (downward)

## 2. 1D Constant Acceleration Kinematics
- $v = u + at$
- $s = ut + \frac{1}{2}at^2$
- $v^2 = u^2 + 2as$
- $s = \left(\frac{u + v}{2}\right)t$

## 3. Work-Energy Theorem & Conservation of Energy
- Kinetic Energy: $K = \frac{1}{2}mv^2$
- Gravitational Potential Energy: $U_g = mgh$
- Elastic Potential Energy (Spring): $U_s = \frac{1}{2}kx^2$
- Work Done by Constant Force: $W = \vec{F} \cdot \Delta\vec{r} = F \Delta r \cos\theta$
- Conservation of Mechanical Energy (Conservative system): $E_{\text{mech}} = K_1 + U_1 = K_2 + U_2$
- Work-Energy Principle (with non-conservative work): $W_{\text{nc}} = \Delta E_{\text{mech}} = (K_2 + U_2) - (K_1 + U_1)$

## 4. Sanity Check Guidelines
- Force unit: Newton $[N] = [\text{kg} \cdot \text{m/s}^2]$
- Energy/Work unit: Joule $[J] = [N \cdot m] = [\text{kg} \cdot \text{m}^2/\text{s}^2]$
- Velocity unit: $[\text{m/s}]$, Acceleration unit: $[\text{m/s}^2]$
